Generous breathing room

The "Lowcountry Light" direction uses very generous spacing throughout -- every section feels open, airy, and inviting. Large padding and a relaxed rhythm echo the unhurried pace of Lowcountry life. Nothing feels cramped or rushed.

Section gaps of 5rem create clear visual separation between content areas, while element gaps of 1.5rem keep related items grouped without crowding. The overall density is generous -- closer to a premium spa website than a dense information portal.

Design Notes

Heading Font

Outfit -- geometric sans with soft, rounded terminals. Modern and warm without being childish. Weights: 300-800.

Body Font

Nunito -- rounded sans-serif with excellent readability. Friendly and approachable at every size. Weights: 400-700.

Color Strategy

60% warm whites and sand / 30% soft grays and navy anchors / 10% teal-blue CTAs. Warm coastal palette, never cold.

Motion

Gentle fade-ups on page load, soft translateY hover lifts on cards, smooth 0.3s transitions. Nothing sharp or sudden.

Surfaces

Rounded corners (10-24px). Soft layered shadows on cards. Gentle gradient backgrounds. Organic curved section dividers.

Accessibility

All text/background pairings verified at WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large text). Both light and dark modes pass.
