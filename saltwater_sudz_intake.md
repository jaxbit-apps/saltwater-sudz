## About / Story
> *Background and story of the business. Includes: why the business was started, mission statement or core values, time in business, certifications, licenses, affiliations, community involvement, and unique selling propositions.*

Owner Ryan Haag graduated from the University of South Carolina in 2021 with an undergraduate degree in Sport and Entertainment Management with a concentration in Business Administration. After spending 1.5 years working at a ballpark and 3.5 years in the corporate world, Ryan and his fiancée relocated to Bluffton, SC, where he launched his dream of owning a detailing business.

Saltwater Sudz has been operating since August 2025 and is growing rapidly. Ryan's mission goes beyond making cars look new — he's focused on building meaningful relationships and genuinely serving his community.

Ryan describes himself as curious, sociable, and tactile — someone who treats detailing as an art form. He uses only top-of-the-line chemicals and is an official partner of **GTechNiq**, a premium brand offering interior, exterior, and ceramic coating products. He plans to complete an accreditation program with GTechNiq in the future.

Saltwater Sudz is **fully licensed and insured in South Carolina**, and Ryan holds **gate passes for Sun City and Palmetto Bluff**.

*"I'm not cheap and nor am I quick, but you can guarantee I'm damn good."*

## Services and Offerings
> *Comprehensive list of services with descriptions and pricing. Includes quote/estimate workflow, warranties or guarantees.*

### Pricing Structure

Ryan quotes every vehicle on-site. Pricing is based on an hourly rate of **$80/hour**:

| Vehicle Type | Estimated Time | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Regular car (normal dirty) | ~3.5 hours | $280 |
| SUV (4-door, 2-row) | ~4 hours | $320 |
| SUV (3-row) or Truck | ~5 hours | $400 |

> **Note:** Ryan frequently goes over estimated time but **never up-charges**. The quoted price is the final price.

### Repeat Customer Discounts

| Frequency | Discount |
|---|---|
| 2x per year or Quarterly | 15% off |
| Bimonthly or Monthly | 20% off |

> Pricing applies whether the service is interior only, exterior only, or both.

### Interior Detail Process

1. **3-Step Floor Mat Cleaning** — Full steam clean, air compressor blow out, and vacuum (carpet or rubber mats)
2. **Full Steam Clean** of interior trim with interior cleaner
3. **Carpet Cleaning** of interior flooring
4. **Leather Shampooing & Conditioning** *(Seat extraction is a separate service — currently unavailable while awaiting a new extractor)*
5. **Door Jamb Cleaning**
6. **Window Cleaning**
7. **Odor Neutralizing**

### Exterior Detail Process

1. Deep wheel and wheel well cleaning
2. Pre-wash
3. Bug removal
4. Contact wash
5. Iron decontamination with clay bar treatment
6. Wash wax
7. Hydrophobic coating finish
8. Wheel shine
