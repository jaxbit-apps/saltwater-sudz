Our Story

From a Passion for Cars to the Lowcountry's Trusted Detailer

Saltwater Sudz was founded by Ryan Haag right here in Bluffton, South Carolina. What started as a deep love for cars and a commitment to doing things the right way has grown into a trusted mobile detailing business serving the entire Lowcountry.

Ryan built Saltwater Sudz the old-fashioned way: through quality work and word of mouth. Every vehicle gets the same level of care and attention to detail, whether it is a daily driver or a weekend showpiece. That approach has earned 15 consecutive five-star Google reviews and a growing base of loyal, repeat customers.

As a locally owned and fully licensed and insured business, Saltwater Sudz is invested in this community. Ryan lives in Bluffton, knows the roads, and understands what Lowcountry conditions do to vehicles -- the salt air, the marsh dust, the relentless summer sun. He has built his service offerings specifically around what local vehicles need.
